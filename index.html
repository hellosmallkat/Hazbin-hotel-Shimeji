Hazbin Hotel

A demonic sanctuary of chaos & redemption

Residents of the Hotel

Perched in Pentagram City within the Pride Ring of Hell, the Hazbin Hotel is a bizarre, vibrant, and wildly eccentric sanctuary. Founded by Princess Charlie Morningstar, it is a hopeful yet misguided rehabilitation centre for damned souls. The building itself is a chaotic amalgamation of whimsical gothic architecture, complete with a carousel, airship remnants, looping train tracks, and Alastor's neon-lit radio tower.

The V Tower

A fortress of surveillance and power, the V Tower is the domain of Vox, Valentino, and Velvette. More than a building, it is a network of hidden cameras, electrified corridors, and completely distinct studios dedicated to media, fashion, and adult entertainment. Often compared to the Tower of Babel, it reflects the Vees' ambition to overthrow the heavens themselves.

Create a Desktop Shortcut

After you download your custom Shimeji:

Windows

  1. Extract the ZIP to a folder you will keep.
  2. Inside that folder, right-click, choose New > Text Document.
  3. Name it Launch_Shimeji.bat (make sure the extension is .bat).
  4. Right-click > Edit, paste:
    java -jar "Shimeji-ee.jar"
  5. Save. Double-click .bat to launch.
  6. For desktop icon: right-click .bat > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

macOS

  1. Extract the ZIP and open the folder.
  2. Open Automator (Command+Space, type Automator).
  3. Choose Application, click Choose.
  4. Add a Run Shell Script action.
  5. Paste:
    cd /path/to/your/extracted/folder
java -jar "Shimeji-ee.jar"
    (Replace with actual path.)
  6. Save to Desktop. Double-click to run.

Tip: Pin the shortcut to your taskbar or dock.
